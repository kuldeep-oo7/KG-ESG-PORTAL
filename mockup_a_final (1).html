Environment Day Celebration 2026

Friday, 5 June 2026
10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
K. Girdharlal Office Premises & Community Green Zone

Join us for a full day of environmental action on World Environment Day. Activities include a green desk decoration competition, a tree plantation drive in the surrounding community, and interactive awareness sessions. All employees and their families are warmly invited to participate and contribute to a greener future.

International Yoga Day 2026

Sunday, 21 June 2026
7:00 AM – 9:00 AM
K. Girdharlal Premises – Open Garden

Celebrate International Yoga Day with a guided morning yoga session open to all employees and their families. Led by a certified instructor, the session will cover yoga postures, pranayama breathing exercises, and mindfulness meditation — supporting holistic health and well-being for all.
