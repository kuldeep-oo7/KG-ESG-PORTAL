FAQs
Frequently Asked Questions
Who can file a complaint under POSH? ▼
Any woman employee — permanent, contractual, temporary, trainee, or visitor — who has experienced sexual harassment at the workplace can file with the ICC. A trusted person may file on her behalf if she is incapacitated.
Is my complaint confidential? ▼
Yes. All complaints and proceedings are strictly confidential. Breach of confidentiality by any party, including ICC members, is a punishable offence under the Act.
What is the time limit to file a complaint? ▼
Within 3 months of the incident, or the last incident in a series. The ICC may extend by another 3 months if circumstances prevented timely filing.
Can I request interim relief during the inquiry? ▼
Yes. You may request transfer, leave of up to 3 months, or restraint orders on the respondent. The ICC grants these at its discretion.
What happens if a complaint is found to be false? ▼
Malicious or knowingly false complaints may result in disciplinary action against the complainant. However, inability to prove a complaint is not considered a false complaint.
Are third-party workers (contractors, vendors) covered? ▼
Yes. The POSH Act covers all women at the workplace, including those employed through third parties such as contractors, vendors, or service providers working on company premises.
